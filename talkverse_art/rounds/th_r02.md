**Style B — soft 3D clay (fixed for all Talkverse apps):** gentle matte clay / soft-3D render, rounded chunky forms, soft studio light, no outlines, pastel palette with the app accent color on clothes or the key object. Characters: friendly modern adults with simple faces (child/parent only when the word says so). Background: very light pastel mesh fading to white (peach · pink · lavender · sky blue blobs), no scenery unless the word needs a place. One clear subject centered, ~70% of the frame, clear margin above the head / around the object. Square 1:1, 1024×1024 or larger, opaque.

- **Every picture must be different** from every other picture in this project: new pose, new composition, new objects, and a **different person each time** (alternate man/woman, vary age 20s–50s, hair style and color, skin tone and faces typical of Thailand, clothing style). Do not reuse the same face or the same outfit. Put the accent color on ONE item only (a scarf, a bag, a cup, a shirt), not on every character's top. Never reuse an earlier layout.
- **Food / drink words:** draw the real dish exactly as it is served in Thailand — correct bowl/plate, correct ingredients and toppings (listed in the hint), realistic portion. Not a generic "food" blob.
- Abstract words (directions, amounts, time, feelings): a clear symbol is fine — arrows, dots, a sun/moon, a face with the emotion — as long as a learner understands it in 2 seconds.
- Verbs: show the action at its most recognizable moment, mid-motion.
- No letters, digits, signs, clocks with numerals, logos, brands, real people, existing characters. Safe for 12+.
- Do not upload failed files or placeholders. Never overwrite; a redo is `_v2`.

**Tone for this block:** warm morning light, background blobs mostly peach and pink, straight-on eye-level view
